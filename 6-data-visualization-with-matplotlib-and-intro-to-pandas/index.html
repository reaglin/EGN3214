Module 6 — Data Visualization with Matplotlib and Intro to Pandas

This module introduces Python-based data visualization using Matplotlib and covers the foundational data-handling capabilities of Pandas. Learners will build skills in creating and styling a variety of plots, organizing multi-panel figures, and performing descriptive analysis on structured datasets — core competencies for engineering data workflows.

This reading introduces learners to Pandas descriptive statistics methods, covering how to compute summary measures such as mean, median, standard deviation, and aggregations on DataFrames, enabling learners to quickly explore and summarize engineering and scientific datasets without writing statistical formulas from scratch.
