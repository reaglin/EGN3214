Module 9 — Engineering Data Analysis and Simulation

This module guides learners through the full engineering data pipeline—from loading and cleaning raw datasets to visualizing results—and introduces numerical simulation techniques for thermal, mechanical, and electrical systems using Python-based ODE methods.
